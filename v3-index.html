Questions, answered

The honest version, not the brochure version.

Who counts as a Dan?

Right now the working rule is simple: Dan, Daniel, or Danny. The full Danstitution, including edge cases, is being written by the founders. Borderline cases stay provisional until it is settled.

Is this a joke?

Yes, and no. It started as a bit. The club, the count, and the gatherings are real. A joke taken seriously is the whole point.

Do I have to live in Austin?

No. Austin is the first chapter because that is where the founders are. The count is global, and chapters open wherever Dans show up.

How do Dan numbers work?

Numbers go out in the order Dans claim them, from one registry, and a number is never edited or reused. A number is official once it is published on the count page.

When do signups open?

Soon. Follow @onlydansclub and you will hear it there first. The button above stays disabled until then, on purpose.

What does it cost?

Joining is free. Hats come later, and nothing is for sale on this page today.
